How It Works

Three straightforward steps to access the Ogun State Cadastral Portal.

1

Register & Verify

Sign up with your Full Name, email, phone number, and address. Click the verification link sent to your email to activate your account.

2

Search Cadastral Parcel

Log in to access the map. Search for specific parcel identifiers (e.g. AB-001) to render and zoom directly to the verified spatial boundaries.

3

Inspect & Save

Review comprehensive ownership and survey details in the dynamic inspector table and save parcels directly to your saved list.
