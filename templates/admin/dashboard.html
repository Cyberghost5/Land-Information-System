Select or Drag & Drop GeoJSON Dataset

Accepts .geojson or .json files containing Polygon or MultiPolygon FeatureCollections.

💡 Expected GeoJSON Properties

The importer extracts standard property keys: Parcel_ID (mandatory), Par_Owner, landuse, Area, Status, Location, LGA, Title_numb, and Surv_plan. Coordinates are preserved and projected on-the-fly.
